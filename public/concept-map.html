Business Logic: Yield Tolerance & Buffer Stock Decision Rules

Rule 1: Standard Tolerance Window (±10.0%)

Formulation vessels naturally fluctuate based on evaporation and clean-out. Target quantities automatically generate an acceptable manufacturing envelope:
Min = Target × 0.90 | Max = Target × 1.10. Batches landing inside this envelope are cleared for standard dispatch without administrative bottleneck.

Rule 2: Actual Delivery vs Fixed Billing

Actual Delivery: Client accepts and pays for the exact produced quantity (e.g. 550 pcs @ ₱180 = ₱99,000).
Fixed PO: Client pays only for the base target (500 pcs @ ₱180 = ₱90,000); surplus overrun (50 pcs) is securely diverted to the client's dedicated Client Buffer Stock vault.

Rule 3: Exception & Overrun Threshold (> +10%)

When yield exceeds +10%, the batch automatically transitions to EXCEPTION_REQUIRES_APPROVAL. The system halts automated DR dispatch until an Admin and the Client provide cryptographic overrun sign-off or allocate the surplus to future buffer stock.
